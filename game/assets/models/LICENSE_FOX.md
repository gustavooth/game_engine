# Fox model attribution

`fox.glb` is the Khronos Group glTF Sample Models "Fox" asset:

- Low poly fox by PixelMannen, released under CC0:
  https://opengameart.org/content/fox-and-shiba
- Rigging and animation by @tomkranis on Sketchfab, released under CC BY 4.0:
  https://sketchfab.com/models/371dea88d7e04a76af5763f2a36866bc
- glTF conversion by @AsoboStudio and @scurest, from the Khronos sample models
  repository:
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/main/2.0/Fox

The model is used only as a test/demo asset.
